Why regular dental check-ups matter

The purpose of check-ups and why intervals vary.

Updated: 21.11.2026 · Oral health information

Check-ups without symptoms

Decay and gum conditions may develop without pain at first. Dental visits help assess oral health and identify needs before problems become more complex.

What may be checked

A dentist may examine teeth, restorations, gums and other oral tissues. X-rays may be considered when needed; not every visit requires identical procedures.

How often should you go?

The right interval varies with decay history, gum condition, dry mouth, diet, medicines and individual risks. A personalised schedule is more useful than a single rule for everyone.

Home care still matters

Regular visits complement, rather than replace, brushing twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and cleaning between teeth.
